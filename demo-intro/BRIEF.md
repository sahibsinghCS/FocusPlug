## Intent

A 15-second cold-open hook for the FocusPlug hackathon demo video. The style is
the classic "headline montage" ad opener: real newspaper and magazine headlines
about declining focus/attention flash one after another, each zoomed in on the
word **focus** (highlighted), swapping every ~1.5s and accelerating. Underneath,
news-anchor voices (switching every couple of seconds, broadcast-filtered)
repeat that focus has become a major issue. Tense, urgent, escalating — then a
hard cut to the FocusPlug beat as the payoff. The user's bar: "elite"; keep
iterating until it is.

## Assets

- None supplied. Headlines must be REAL published headlines/findings about
  declining attention (e.g. Gloria Mark's 47-second average screen attention,
  the TIME/Microsoft goldfish study, Johann Hari's "Stolen Focus" coverage),
  attributed to their real publications as citation-style article snippets.

## Customizations

- News-anchor audio bed: 3–4 generated anchor lines in different voices,
  broadcast/radio EQ, channel-switch static between voice swaps.
- Tense rising music/pulse bed under the whole hook.
- End beat: montage cuts out → FocusPlug title beat (the only resolution).

## Notes

- This is NOT part of the FocusPlug codebase — do not modify any repo source
  files. The project lives in demo-intro/ only.
- 16:9 1920x1080, plays on a projector at the Hyperbloom hackathon.
- Headlines must be real things — no invented publications or fake quotes.
